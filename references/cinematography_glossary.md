# Cinematography & Visual Glossary for REA

Use these precise cinematography terms when deconstructing videos into AI prompts.

---

## 1. Camera Movements

| Movement | Description | AI Prompt Keyword |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Dolly In / Out** | Moving physical camera closer to or further from subject | `Slow dolly in`, `Dolly push forward` |
| **Pan** | Rotating camera horizontally on fixed axis | `Pan right`, `Pan left`, `Whip pan` |
| **Tilt** | Pivoting camera vertically up or down | `Tilt up to sky`, `Tilt down from ceiling` |
| **Pedestal / Boom** | Moving entire camera vertically up or down | `Pedestal shot rising`, `Crane shot descending` |
| **Truck / Track** | Moving camera sideways parallel to subject | `Lateral tracking shot`, `Truck left` |
| **Arc / Orbit** | Revolving camera around subject in a circle | `360 orbital camera`, `Semi-arc camera shot` |
| **Roll / Dutch Angle** | Tilting horizon off-level for tension or style | `Dutch angle`, `Subtle barrel roll` |
| **Dolly Zoom (Vertigo)** | Dolly in while zooming out (or vice versa) | `Vertigo dolly zoom effect` |
| **FPV Drone** | High-speed, agile acrobatic camera flight | `Dynamic FPV drone shot`, `Fly-through shot` |
| **Handheld Shake** | Natural subtle organic camera shake | `Organic handheld camera`, `Documentary style shake` |

## 2. Shot Framing & Lenses

- **Extreme Close-Up (ECU)**: Focus on eyes, lips, fine textures, or product dials.
- **Close-Up (CU)**: Head and shoulders; emotional focus.
- **Medium Shot (MS)**: Waist up; natural human interaction.
- **Full Shot (FS) / Cowboy**: Whole body visible in environment.
- **Establishing Wide Shot (WS)**: Shows entire location, architecture, scale.
- **Lenses**:
  - `14mm - 20mm Ultra-wide`: Exaggerated perspective, expansive vistas, dynamic speed.
  - `24mm - 35mm Wide`: Standard cinematic documentary and narrative shot.
  - `50mm Standard`: Natural human eye perspective, zero optical distortion.
  - `85mm - 135mm Telephoto / Portrait`: Compression of background, creamy shallow depth of field (bokeh).
  - `Anamorphic Lens`: Oval bokeh, horizontal blue/gold streak flares, 2.39:1 widescreen ratio.
  - `Macro Lens`: 1:1 reproduction of microscopic details (dew drops, fabric weave, circuit boards).

---

## 3. Lighting Styles & Direction

- **Volumetric / God Rays**: Beams of light piercing through dust, smoke, or blinds (`volumetric god rays streaming through window`).
- **Rim Light / Backlight**: Light placed behind subject creating an outline separating them from background (`subtle rim light highlighting silhouette`).
- **Chiaroscuro / Low-Key**: High contrast between deep shadow and intense pool of light (`chiaroscuro lighting, deep noir shadows`).
- **High-Key / Commercial Clean**: Evenly distributed, bright, shadowless lighting (`high-key clean Apple commercial studio lighting`).
- **Practical Lighting**: Real light sources visible inside the scene (lamps, neon signs, laptop screens).
- **Golden Hour & Blue Hour**: Warm sunset glow (`golden hour warm amber rays`) or post-sunset cool ambient tone (`moody blue hour twilight`).
