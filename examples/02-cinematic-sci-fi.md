# Case Study 02: Cinematic Sci-Fi Scene REA

## Input
- **Scenario**: A hyper-realistic sci-fi cinematic sequence featuring a lone astronaut exploring an alien cavern with bioluminescent flora.

---

## 1. Pipeline Classification
- **Primary Archetype**: Pure Generative AI Video (Text-to-Video via Runway Gen-3 Alpha or Kling AI v1.5 Pro).
- **Aspect Ratio**: 2.39:1 (Anamorphic widescreen).

---

## 2. Visual & Cinematographic Ingredients
- **Subject**: Female astronaut in a weathered white EVA spacesuit with subtle golden helmet reflection.
- **Lighting**: Deep chiaroscuro; bioluminescent cyan and electric violet light emitted from alien fungal pods on the cavern walls; soft specular helmet visor rim light.
- **Optics & Lens**: Anamorphic 35mm lens, subtle horizontal streak flare, oval bokeh in out-of-focus background particles.
- **Camera Vector**: Low-angle forward dolly track with slight counter-clockwise roll (Dutch tilt) moving slowly past the astronaut's shoulder.

---

```text
Cinematic low-angle camera slowly pushing forward through a cavernous alien cave, revealing an astronaut in a worn white EVA suit gazing at glowing turquoise bioluminescent flora. Soft volumetric mist, anamorphic lens flares, 35mm film grain, 8k cinematic realism.
```
